Heart Attack Response

Signs: Chest pain, shortness of breath, nausea, cold sweats

Actions:

  • Call 911 immediately
  • Have person sit and rest
  • Loosen tight clothing
  • Give aspirin if available and not allergic
  • Monitor breathing and pulse
  • Be ready to perform CPR

Stroke Response

FAST Test:

  • Face - Drooping on one side
  • Arms - Weakness or numbness
  • Speech - Slurred or confused
  • Time - Call 911 immediately

Actions:

  • Note time symptoms started
  • Keep person calm and still
  • Don't give food or drink

Anaphylaxis Response

Signs: Difficulty breathing, swelling, hives, rapid pulse, dizziness

Actions:

  • Call 911 immediately
  • Use EpiPen if available
  • Have person lie down
  • Elevate legs if possible
  • Monitor breathing
  • Be ready for CPR

Head Injury Response

Warning Signs: Loss of consciousness, confusion, vomiting, unequal pupils

Actions:

  • Call 911 for serious injuries
  • Keep person still
  • Don't move if neck injury suspected
  • Apply ice to swelling
  • Monitor for changes
  • Watch for 24-48 hours

Fracture Response

Signs: Severe pain, swelling, deformity, inability to move

Actions:

  • Don't move injured area
  • Immobilize with splint
  • Apply ice (not directly)
  • Elevate if possible
  • Call 911 for severe fractures
  • Treat for shock if needed

Heat Stroke Response

Signs: High body temp, confusion, rapid pulse, hot dry skin

Actions:

  • Call 911 immediately
  • Move to cool area
  • Remove excess clothing
  • Cool with water/ice packs
  • Fan the person
  • Monitor temperature

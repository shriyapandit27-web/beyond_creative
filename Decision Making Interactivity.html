Scenario 1: Collapsed Runner

You're at a community park when you see a runner suddenly collapse on the jogging path. As you approach, you notice the person is lying motionless on their back. Several bystanders are standing around looking concerned but no one has taken action yet.
What should be your FIRST action?
A. Immediately start chest compressions
B. Check if the scene is safe, then check for responsiveness
C. Call 911 and wait for paramedics to arrive
D. Give the person water and help them sit up

Scenario 2: Restaurant Emergency

You're dining at a restaurant when you hear a commotion at a nearby table. A woman is standing up, clutching her throat with both hands. Her face is turning red and she appears unable to speak or cough effectively. Her companion is calling for help.
What is the most appropriate immediate action?
A. Encourage her to keep coughing forcefully
B. Perform the Heimlich maneuver (abdominal thrusts)
C. Give her water to help wash down the obstruction
D. Lay her down and perform CPR

Scenario 3: Car Accident

What should you advise the injured driver to do?
A. Help them exit the vehicle carefully to assess injuries
B. Tell them to stay in the vehicle and call 911 immediately
C. Give them pain medication and help them walk around
D. Apply ice to their neck and let them rest in the car

Scenario 4: Kitchen Burn

What is the best immediate first aid for this burn?

B. Apply butter or oil to protect the skin
C. Run cool (not cold) water over the burn for 10-20 minutes
D. Pop the blisters to release pressure and apply ointment

A. Apply a tourniquet immediately above the wound
B. Put on gloves, apply direct pressure with gauze, and elevate the arm
C. Clean the wound thoroughly with water before applying pressure
